About Marico: 
Marico (BSE: 531642, NSE: “MARICO”) is one of India’s leading consumer goods companies operating in the global 
beauty and wellness space. During 2020-21, Marico recorded a turnover of INR 80.5 billion (USD 1.1 billion) through 
its products sold in India and chosen markets in Asia and Africa.
