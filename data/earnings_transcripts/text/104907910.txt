Bharat Puri:

price at the end of March, in the last week of March and we are taking price again in this 
month, which is in the month of May. We will cover to the extent of about 75% of inflation.

result of a number of factors, which includes the U.S. storms and therefore, closures in the

in the second half of the year, this will moderate. We will keep a close watch. But yes, as 
far as the first 6 months is concerned, this is definitely going to have a substantial impact on 
our operations, and therefore, we have had to take price.
