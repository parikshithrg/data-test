revenue. 
The flywheel for both is very simple. Every patient who recovers and leaves our hospital takes 
a medicine regimen that provides recurring product revenue for months or years. Every medicine 
customer who calls our support center is a potential IPD admission.  
The more healthy people come to us, the more they become mouthpieces for future patients.

INR60 lakh per camp.
