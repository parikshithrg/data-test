increased significantly in NCR, which contributes nearly 50% to our total portfolio. The 
minimum wage increase has been to the tune of about 35% to 40%, spanning over unskilled and 
skilled categories.

increase in our staff costs, where we have significantly increased our employee base as we have 
strengthened the organization, mobilized staff resources for our enlarged project portfolio. The 
net order book of the company as on 30th June is INR20,663.52 crores to be executed over the
