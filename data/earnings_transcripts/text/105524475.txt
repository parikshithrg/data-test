Q4FY23 revenue is at ₹ 159 crores, which has grown year-to-year by 2%, and revenue for the full

the fiscal year, PAT was at ₹ 17.5 crores, doubling more as compared to FY22 with spectacular year-
on-year growth of 171%. 
Now moving on to some segmental highlights.

institutional channels contributed to 34% and 3%, respectively, to the total revenue. Our presence 
continues to strengthen in Tier II and Tier III cities as we continue to add stores in these regions. In 
this fiscal year, we opened 47 new stores in Tier III cities, followed by 17 stores in Tier I regions and 
27 stores in metro and Tier II cities.

respectively. We currently operate 74% of stores through franchise models, which includes EBOs, 
BOs and FRM stores while 26% of the stores are operated through Company-Owned Outlets.
