benefit of the IT Act as far as our tax calculations are concerned and then arriving at the PBT 
and PAT, right. But then Companies Act provides a lower depreciation and IT Act can provide 
a higher depreciation so there is always a difference between the two and the difference is 
accounted for by way of this deferred tax liability in the books. It‟s only a book entry that is all 
I would say.
