Scheme, PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana add about 200 gigawatts of demand over the coming 
years.

and self-reliance. Energy independence is no longer spoken of only in terms of coal reserves or 
imported oil, it is now defined by our ability to build, innovate and manufacture at a resilient 
renewable energy ecosystem right here in India. This trend that we are seeing is a multi-decadal trend 
that we are just getting started with.
