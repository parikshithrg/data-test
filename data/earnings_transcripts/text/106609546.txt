Financial and operational performance. The fourth quarter concluded a year of stabilization and 
strengthening fundamentals. Momentum improved sequentially through the year, with Q4 
reflecting operational stability across both API and finished dosage businesses, improved 
compliance readiness and consistent execution against stated priorities.

remediation expenditure, underscoring the benefits of a diversified footprint, disciplined 
execution and improved business resilience.

with all action point responses submitted in February and regular progress updates maintained 
thereafter.
