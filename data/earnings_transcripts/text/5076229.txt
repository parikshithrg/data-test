first quarter of the FY2023 of Valiant Organics Limited. Thank you for joining us today and 
hope everyone is keeping safe and well. I hope you had a chance to review the earnings 
presentations and our finances that we have posted on our website and exchanges. On a 
consolidated basis for the first quarter of FY2022-2023 the revenue from operations stood 
at around Rs.266 Crores which grew by about 9% on year-on-year basis and EBITDA was 
about Rs.32 Crores which degrew by about 37% year-on-year. With EBITDA margins 
reported at 11.99% the net profit for the quarter was about Rs.17 Crores which decreased by 
about 41% year-on-year with PAT margins reported at 6.35%. 
 
You can see our Q1 FY2023 financials were significantly impacted primarily due to the 
unfortunate blast at our Chlorination plant in Sarigam on June 3, 2022 leading to complete 
plant shutdown for an extensive period. The blast was an isolated incident that occurred in 
one of our chlorination reactors which caused minor damage. Thankfully there was no loss 
to human life; however, a minor injury was reported to one person who received the 
medical treatment and has been recovered. The incident was reported to concerned 
authorities immediately and after extensive checks the extent of the damage has been 
estimated to around Rs.5 Crores which will be fully covered under the insurance along with 
LOP. The plant was given permission to resume operations on July 21, 2022 although it 
would take few weeks to restore operations to resume normalcy. Unfortunately last quarter 
apart from this unfortunate event at Sarigam we had a double whammy impact across some
