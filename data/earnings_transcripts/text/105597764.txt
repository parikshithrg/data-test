FY23 was a good year for us in terms of sales, profitability and store expansion. Our sales grew

this financial year as well. We are able to maintain quarterly business performance mainly 
because we are able to offer products within an affordable price range. The softening of raw

100 more retail stores by FY25. Our distribution network is getting stronger as we added 44 new 
distributors in Q1, taking our total count to 732 distributors. Our retail and distribution business 
are currently present in 27 states and 4 union territories as of June 2023.

business as a going concern into a Company to be incorporated as a wholly-owned subsidiary of 
Khadim India Limited as of now. Going forward, the new entity would function as an
