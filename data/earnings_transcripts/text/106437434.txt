About Rane Group  
Headquartered in Chennai, India, Rane Group is engaged primarily in manufacturing auto 
components for well over six decades. Through its group companies, it supplies Steering and 
Suspension Systems, Brake Components, Engine Components, Occupant Safety Systems 
and Light Metal Casting Components to major OEMs and Aftermarket in India and abroad. 
Its products serve a variety of industry segments including Passenger Vehicles, Commercial 
Vehicles, Farm Tractors, Two-wheelers, Three-wheelers, Railways and Stationary Engines.
