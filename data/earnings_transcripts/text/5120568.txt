year. Our balance sheet remains robust and we continue to enjoy a net debt free status. We have a 
cash balance of Rs.277 Crores which comprises cash and bank balances along with current and 
non-current investments. Long-term borrowing is 6.7 Crores as of September 2022 compared to 
Rs.8.3 Crores as of March 2022, which shows our efficiency in serving the debt.

with a well-diversified product portfolio such as cotton, woollen, kids, and home furnishing.
