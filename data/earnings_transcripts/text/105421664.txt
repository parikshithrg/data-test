the expansion of our supplier base has helped in the stability of raw-material supplies.

had previously indicated, we anticipate an improvement during the second-half.

producing positive results, coupled with some external factors that input prices softening, we 
expect a quarter-on-quarter steady improvement. We have had an improvement in performance 
sequentially and are working towards having a stronger end to the year, despite some macro-
level volatility caused by global uncertainties.

positive tailwinds for future growth. Margins continued to be impacted as expected and outlined 
in the previous call due to steep increase in cost of raw materials, solvents and utilities. Also,
