• Consolidated Revenue for Q4 FY 14 Y-o-Y decreased by 3.3% in USD terms and increased by 10.6% in INR terms,
consolidated operating revenue in USD terms decreased by -0.2% in current quarter, over previous quarter. In INR
terms, consolidated revenues decreased by -0.7% over previous quarter .
• In USD terms, Q4 FY 14 consolidated (excluding 3D PLM) operating revenues for the quarter increased by 2.4% in
USD terms and increased by 2.1% in INR terms. It decreased by 4.5% Y-o-Y, in terms of USD & increased by 9.2%
in INR terms.
• Effective tax rate for the quarter is 40.78%.
• EPS for the quarter at INR 1.06 is lower by 10.6% over previous quarter primarily on account of lower Revenue and
investments in Sales Transformation exercise. Over corresponding quarter in previous year, EPS is lower by 39.8%.
• EPS after adjusting for extraordinary items is INR 2.72 as against INR 1.28 in Q3 14 and INR 1.90 in Q4 13; this
includes a one-time cost towards consultant fees of INR 60 Mn.
• New contracts amounting to USD 10.49 Mn won during the quarter.
• Recommended dividend of INR 2 on face value of INR 2 per share, by the Board of Directors.
• Total headcount is increased from 4395 in Q3 14 to 4401 in Q4 14.

MIS format is different from the income statement published as part of financial results in 
terms of grouping of cost elements.
Key differences to be noted are as follows:
1.
MIS format breaks-up operating costs as COR, S&M and G&A
2.
Depreciation line item in MIS format also includes the lease rentals for computing
hardware (CAPEX equivalent) and excludes software depreciation which is clubbed 
under software expenses in COR
3.
FX gain/(loss) is grouped under Other Income in MIS format as against operating cost 
in financials.
4.
MIS Format provides only interest expenses whereas financial results clubs interest 
and bank charges in one line item.
For EBITDA calculation, amortization software has been added and lease rentals
have been reduced in depreciation

Note from the MD’s Desk 
 
FY 14 has been a year of transformation wherein we have invested heavily in building a 
scalable enterprise. At the same time, we have been tested by a substantial decline in 
revenue from one of our largest customers; I am happy to report that we believe we have 
seen the worst in that situation while we experienced growth from many others. 
 
I thought it appropriate to use this opportunity to look back on the last twelve months, and 
highlight some of the transformations we have put in place in the context of the future.  
 
We started the year by bringing focus to our accounts. We reduced the number of 
customers we served from around 140, down to just over 70, our objective being to be able 
to spend more time to understand the key drivers of their business and propose innovative 
solutions for their problems. From 1st April, we shifted P&L responsibility to our verticals 
with a view to make them more accountable as well as to build competency in management, 
thus increasing our talent pool. 
 
In April 2013, we commenced our journey to re-implement our ERP backbone sticking to the 
‘out of the box’ approach. A sound information backbone is an essential step to scale, as 
otherwise too much is done manually, therefore person dependent and prone to error as 
well as delay. The approach necessitated adoption of processes and best practices as 
supported by the software. In turn, it has meant we have had to change our organization to 
support these processes. We believe these changes in the organization will help us to drive 
competency enhancement throughout our workforce leading to improved technical 
capability and lower attrition; indeed the improvement in our ability to enhance 
competency was a key factor in our decision to implement OOTB. The new ERP went live on 
3rd February, worldwide; as expected we face teething issues, which will take some time to 
settle down. This is the first time the entire corporation is on one system! 
 
We initiated and completed a comprehensive sales training program to enable our sales 
team to ‘value sell’ rather than just take orders. The methodology requires us to map the 
customer (which fits in with our focused approach), the opportunity and provides a series of 
metrics, which help us to more scientifically gauge progress and take corrective, steps in a 
planned manner. Most important it ensures the entire sales team talks ‘one language’, so 
sharing of learning’s and best practices becomes possible. 
 
We refined our offerings to focus on six, which we believe help to differentiate us, add value 
to customers, and bring about sustainable revenues. The ‘go to market’ and delivery metrics 
for each of them will undergo continuous ‘upgrades’ as we move forward. The methodology 
we adopted, with the help of external consultants, will enable us to build more such winning 
solutions. In all of this, we are continuously intertwining our own technology, enhancing it 
while creating new IP, all adding to the uniqueness of Geometric. Finally, we set up a 
program to intensively support ‘large deals’, ensuring attention at top management level for 
removing bottlenecks and sustaining momentum. 
 
The underlying objective for all of these actions is to build a sustainable, scalable growth 
engine. I am pleased to report that the early signs are positive. Our large deal closure rate 
improved over the previous quarter from 21% to 35%, and doubled compared to H1 of last 
fiscal. We embarked this transformation by identifying 22 important deals beginning of last 

 
 
of quarter, out of which we won 3 deals, lost 2 deals, carried forwarded 15 deals and 
dropped two. Our new business pipeline has shown steady growth, up 15% over the 
previous quarter. I am also pleased to report that we have resumed on our path to growth. 
Our revenues (excluding our joint venture, 3DPLM) grew in USD terms about 2.5% Q-Q and 
3% Q-Q in constant currency terms. As guided last quarter, the results include an 
extraordinary cost towards consultants of about  ` 6 crores. 
 
Our change in approach from execution to creating solutions with focus on a select group of 
customers I believe is starting to pay off. We are beginning to get invited to participate in 
larger opportunities as companies appreciate our attention and desire to understand their 
business so as to help them by solving problems, improving productivity, reducing time to 
market and so on. Today we are making it to ‘lists’ of participants, short lists and we hope as 
these opportunities mature, to wins. 
 
We made substantial changes in FY 14, which will take some time to settle down. The 
emphasis now is on execution; as in any transformation, there will be setbacks, need for 
refinement and the like. Nonetheless, we look at FY 15 with a sense of optimism as we do 
see traction directly linked to the direction we have taken. We believe we can match 
industry growth, which in turn will enable us to improve our margins as we leverage the 
investments we have made in FY 14. I must note, however our joint venture, 3DPLM is likely, 
on an annual basis, to be flat as the effect of the lower ` / $ conversion rate kicks in. 
Nonetheless, our goal is to grow each quarter and set the base for outgrowing the industry 
in the years to come. 
 
The proof of the pudding, as always, will be in the eating 
