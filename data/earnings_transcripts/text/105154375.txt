during H1 of this year compared to H2 of last year. There has been a sharp increase in the 
price of motors, cables, accessories, transport, and electrical panels because of increase in 
copper and aluminium prices.

in having adequate skilled manpower at project sites in spite of offering higher 
compensation and arranging transport and other facilities. Some difficulty continues in the

last year, we had salary cuts in ISGEC Engineering Limited and ISGEC Hitachi Zosen

this quarter as well as half year.
