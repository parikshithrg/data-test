crores, our highest ever for quarter 1. This has been up by 53% as compared to quarter 1 FY 
'25. Our EBITDA stood at Rs. 6.76 crores for quarter 1 FY '26 against Rs. 4.95 crores for quarter 
1 FY '25. Our profit after tax stood at Rs. 3.15 crores, up from Rs. 2.4 crores in quarter 1 FY '25. 
Results have been improving, indicating our focus to capitalize on India's pharma promotion 
sector potential.
