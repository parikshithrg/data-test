that the Government has prescribed a national floor minimum wage, which says that now the 
States cannot arbitrarily keep their minimum wage low, so as to keep the cost of production 
lower. For example, a lot of you will be surprised that the minimum wage of an industrialized

10,000 per month. Now, this disparity was a historical anomaly in the labor markets of this 
country, which are now being rationalized. So, when you talk about quote-on-wages and the
