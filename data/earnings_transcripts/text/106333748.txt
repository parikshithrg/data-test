the previous year quarter.

operations basis only has improved from 4.5% to 6.2% because of managing 
efficiencies on expenses and new restaurants contributing to the bottom line. One 
major expenditure, which we continue to incur is spends on aggregator platforms, 
which is almost to the tune of 5% of the revenues.
