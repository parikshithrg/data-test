UltraTech Cement has grown at 9.7% Y-o-Y, including Kesoram in both the periods, though for

NCLT scheme, we have consolidated the financials of Kesoram in the last reported quarter, 
which is April-June '24 which also has been recasted to include it in our results.

waves, but the heat waves caused a lot of turmoil in the initial part of the quarter. The monsoons 
now having spread across the country will be good for the rural market. Rural markets continue 
to be doing their swan song and we expect them to grow favorably in the future months and 
future quarters.

number of deals being signed up, land purchases already registered and transactions being 
undertaken, clearly speak about a rebound, which should be visible in the future quarters in the 
redevelopment market. The redevelopment market remains strong for Mumbai and Pune, and 
we are seeing continuous launches of new projects. These new projects, which get launched, 
would see cement consumption commencing only after 12 months to 18 months of their launch.

further with WHRS installations.
