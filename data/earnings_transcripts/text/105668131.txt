The Indian economy is picking up steam and strength despite the global challenges.  
Real GDP growth for Q1 FY24 came in at 7.8% YoY significantly led by domestic drivers of

The momentum in agricultural activity in Q2 FY24 has been sustained, although the monsoon

Looking internally now at Nuvoco's performance for the quarter ended 30th September 2023.

network. Moreover, as you know, cement prices in the East also improved in September’23.

continues to be robust with ~14% YoY growth. In the East, core markets of West Bengal and 
Bihar saw subdued growth during the quarter. However, we continue to hold ground and remain
