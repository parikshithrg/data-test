to our customers.

as the guest of honor at the celebrations on July 1st to commemorate the 40th anniversary of our group 
company, Bharat Seats.

sustainable value for all our stakeholders. The auto and auto component industry is going through 
exciting times and we believe there is considerable opportunity for an entity with quality operations
