I will quickly give you a summary of the Financial Performance for the year and the quarter,

A quick look at the full year financials:
Consolidated revenue for FY22-23 came in at Rs. 11,752 crores, so obviously a great milestone 
for the Company and was up by 18.9% over the previous year. This was driven by strong volume 
growth, improved mix and price increases. Growth was broad based across the Consumer and 
Bazaar and the B2B segments with the Domestic Consumer and Bazaar segment reporting 
growth in excess of 20%.
Consolidated margins came in at 242 basis points lower, largely on account of the escalation
input costs which were pronounced in the first half of last year. These increases in input cost 
were partially mitigated by pricing, improved mix and cost reduction initiatives.
Consolidated EBITDA came in just a shade under Rs. 2,000 crores and at Rs. 1,984 crores; was 
up by 7.4% over last year.
Standalone for Pidilite Industries Ltd. revenues were Rs. 10,545 crores which grew by 19.1%. 
Again, the growth was led by a very strong 20.9% growth in the Consumer and Bazaar segment 
whereas the B2B segment grew by 12.4%.

more layer in the distribution chain which they called as Authorised Dealers (ADs), which stood

scope to do other businesses so on and so forth, but frankly this layer was unnecessary in the 
distribution change. It is good for a multinational that does not have a bazaar or a consumer 
presence. So, we basically removed one layer. So, in the last two months of the year in February 
and March, basically we sold all of the stocks which were at the AD end because now there will 
be no ADs from first of April and therefore our primary sales in Araldite was impacted 
substantially in the fourth quarter.
