Sandeep Batra: 
Good evening everyone. Joining me today for this call is our Group Chief Financial Officer- 
Anindya Banerjee. Thank you all for joining us today. We hope that you are safe and in 
good health. 
The Indian economy has continued to show resilience amidst a volatile global 
environment. The underlying growth momentum is visible in increasing steel and cement 
output, GST collections, capacity utilisations, rising demand for electricity and travel. The 
Government led capex cycle is continuing. Financial stability has been maintained and 
inflation, though elevated, has moderated from its peak. We will continue to monitor 
these developments closely. 
Our strategic focus is growing our risk-calibrated core operating profit through the 360-
degree customer centric approach and by serving opportunities across customer 
segments and ecosystems. We continue to operate within our strategic framework and 
strengthen our franchise, enhance our delivery and servicing capabilities and expand our 
technology and digital offerings. 
Our Board has today approved the financial results of ICICI Bank for the quarter ended 
March 31, 2023. I would like to highlight some key numbers: 
A. Profit and capital 
1. Core operating profit, that is, profit before provisions and tax, excluding treasury 
income, grew by 36.4% year-on-year to  ₹ 13,866 crore in Q4-2023.  
2. Net interest income increased by 40.2% year-on-year to ₹ 17,667 crore in Q4-
2023. 
3. The net interest margin was 4.90% in Q4-2023 compared to 4.65% in Q3-2023 
and 4.00% in Q4-2022. Net interest margin was 4.48% in FY2023. 
4. Fee income grew by 10.6% year-on-year to ₹ 4,830 crore in Q4-2023. 
5. Core operating profit less provisions grew by 34.7% year-on-year to ₹ 12,247 
crore in Q4-2023. 
6. The profit after tax grew by 30.0% year-on-year to ₹ 9,122 crore in Q4-2023. 
7. The consolidated profit after tax grew by 27.6% year-on-year to ₹ 9,853 crore 
in Q4-2023  
8. The standalone RoE was 18.9% in Q4-2023. 

Page 3 of 20 
 
9. At March 31, 2023, the Bank had a net worth over ₹ 2.0 lakh crore. Including 
profits for FY2023 and after reckoning the impact of proposed dividend, CET-1 
ratio was 17.12%, Tier 1 ratio was 17.60% and total capital adequacy ratio was 
18.34%. 
 
B.  Deposit growth 
1. Total period-end deposits increased by 10.9% year-on-year to ₹ 11,80,841 crore 
at March 31, 2023. 
2. Period-end term deposits increased by 17.1% year-on-year to ₹ 6,39,579 crore at 
March 31, 2023. 
3. Average current account deposits increased by 9.3% year-on-year. 
4. Average savings account deposits increased by 7.5% year-on-year. 
5. We opened about 480 branches during FY2023 and had a network of 5,900 
branches and 16,650 ATMs/cash recycling machines at March 31, 2023. 
6. The Bank added about 23,200 employees in the last 12 months and had about 
129,000 employees at March 31, 2023. 
 
C.  Loan growth  
1. The overall loan portfolio grew by 18.7% year-on-year and 4.7% sequentially at 
March 31, 2023. The domestic loan portfolio grew by 20.5% year-on-year and 
5.0% sequentially at March 31, 2022.  
2. The retail loan portfolio grew by 22.7% year-on-year and 5.4% sequentially. 
Including non-fund outstanding, the retail loan portfolio was 45.7% of the total 
portfolio. The rural portfolio grew by 13.8% year-on-year and 5.5% sequentially. 
The business banking portfolio grew by 34.9% year-on-year and 7.8% 
sequentially. The SME business, comprising borrowers with a turnover of less than 
₹ 250 crore, grew by 19.2% year-on-year and 6.2% sequentially. Growth in the 
domestic corporate portfolio was 21.2% year-on-year and 3.8% sequentially at 
March 31, 2023. 
3. 73.5% of the total loan portfolio, excluding, retail and rural, was rated A- and 
above at March 31, 2023

D. Digital initiatives  
1. Leveraging digital and technology across businesses is a key element of our 
strategy of growing the risk-calibrated core operating profit. We continue to see 
increasing adoption and usage of our digital platforms by our customers.  
 
2. There have been more than 90 lakh activations of iMobile Pay by non-ICICI Bank 
account holders as of end-March. The value of transactions by non-ICICI Bank 
account holders in Q4 of this year was 1.3 times the value of transactions in Q4 
of last year. 
 
3. The value of the Bank’s merchant acquiring transactions through UPI grew by 
20% sequentially and 112% year-on-year in Q4-2023. The Bank had a market 
share of about 30% by value in electronic toll collections through FASTag in Q4-
2023, with an 18% year-on-year growth in collections in Q4-2023. 
 
4. The business banking and SME franchise continues to grow on the back of digital 
offerings and platforms like InstaBIZ along with the Bank’s extensive branch 
network. The value of financial transactions on InstaBIZ grew by about 22% year-
on-year in FY2023. There have been about 225,000 registrations from non-ICICI 
Bank account holders on InstaBIZ till March 31, 2023. 
 
5. The Bank has created more than 20 industry specific STACKs which provide 
bespoke and purpose-based digital solutions to corporate clients and their 
ecosystems. The Bank’s Trade Online and Trade Emerge platforms allow 
customers to perform most of their trade finance and foreign exchange 
transactions digitally. About 70% of trade transactions were done digitally in Q4-
2023. The value of transactions done through these platforms in Q4-2023 was 
1.7 times the value of transactions in Q4-2022. 
 
6. During the quarter, the Bank launched an array of digital solutions for capital 
market participants and clients of custody services. The solutions enable various 
participants including brokers, Portfolio Management Service providers, Foreign 
Portfolio Investors, Foreign Direct Investors and Alternative Investment Funds to 
seamlessly meet all their banking requirements.

The total fund based outstanding to all borrowers under resolution as per the
