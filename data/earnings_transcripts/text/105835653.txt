on-year increase. Black Fort grew by 50% and Power Cool saw a 42% volume increase. 
Total IMFL volume for the financial year FY24 was 10 lakh cases as compared to 8.9 
lakh cases in financial year FY23. The overall IMFL portfolio grew by 14%. These 
remarkable strides were propelled by robust demand for our products and our 
commitment to delivering top-notch offerings tailored to our consumers' preferences.
