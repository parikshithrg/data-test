Despite a weaker export market and ongoing pressure from low-priced imports, we achieved

supported by ambitious infrastructure projects; increased adoption of stainless steel in the 
process industry; and rising importance of life cycle costs in public sector procurement. All these 
factors point to continued demand for stainless steel in the country. 
 
On the export front, our volumes have remained subdued due to the ongoing geopolitical
