both sales and non-sales functions. In certain cases, automation has allowed us to manage 
desired output with lesser headcount. And in sales, we have tried to rationalize some low 
productivity workforce. Overall, on employee count focus was on maximizing productivity 
and bringing in efficiencies across functions.

expenses. Advertising expenses stood at about INR5 crores for the quarter.

expect basis net yield to maturity of about 7.2% of our treasury portfolio. Bond yields during 
the last quarter were quite stable. Profit before taxes stood at INR120.9 crores, growing 38% 
year-on-year. Effective tax rate stood at 23.9%, which is presently on higher side since bulk of 
our treasury mark-to-market gains are currently short term in nature. And hence, provisioning 
for taxes happens at full tax rate. Once they cross 3-year holding period, higher tax provisions 
will get reversed.

17.5% year-on-year. Active paid campaigns at the end of the quarter stood at about 567,000,
