due to transition to an auction regime in the wind sector.

facilities in Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh. We delivered 90-megawatts and

on, slide #7 and 8 of the presentation, which gives details of our focus on balance sheet improvement

fact that working capital level remains the same even after the ramping up of operations. The net debt

receivable number is optically higher but it includes the quarterly sale of Rs.437 Crores as well as 
significant portion of Q1 revenue.

back of better coordinated production, inventory planning and execution of wind projects.
