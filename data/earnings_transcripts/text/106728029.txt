36%, translating into an EBITDA of INR87 crores and PAT of INR 73 crores. 
 
The Q4 FY26 EBITDA and PAT after adjusting the one-off operating expense transactions is 
roughly INR 83 crores and INR 53 crores, respectively. Thereby, the current quarter EBITDA 
and PAT reflected a growth of 5% and 37%, respectively. 
 
The Q-on-Q increase in revenue was primarily led by improved realization in all the segments. 
Speaking on Y-o-Y comparison, the sales were moderated by 6% during the quarter. The 
revenue moderation was primarily led by decrease in sales volume, which was on account of 
supply side headwinds as mentioned earlier. The demand environment continues to be steady.

and sustainable profit improvement. HALS is now 22% of our sales, and we have been able to
