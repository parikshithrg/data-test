prioritizing newer technologies in infusion bags.

business revenue.

Ophthalmic solution and Phytonadione injectable emulsion. This brings our cumulative ANDA 
filings in the U.S. to 366, with 312 approvals and 54 pending. Globally, we now hold an 
impressive 1,736 product registrations, demonstrating our ongoing commitment to expanding 
access to high-quality medicines.
