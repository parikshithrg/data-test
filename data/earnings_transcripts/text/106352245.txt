envisioned an ambitious goal of achieving 500 GW of renewable capacity by 2030 and net-zero 
emissions by 2070. This scale-up is historic and hence requires strong grid infrastructure and 
massive energy storage deployment. As renewable penetration rises, the new growth frontier
