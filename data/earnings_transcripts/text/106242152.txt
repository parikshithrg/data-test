managed to grow our PAT by 12.9% year-over-year, achieving margin expansion and 
operational efficiency. This performance was largely driven by our strong execution strategy, 
continued demand during the festive season and wedding season and uptick in consumer 
sentiments and our ongoing efforts to enhance our product portfolio. 
For FY '25, our revenue per store stands at Rs. 145 crores, while net profit per store reached 
almost Rs. 4.11 crores, demonstrating operational efficiency and profitability. Our same store 
growth SSSG stood strong at 26.5% and that keeps the strong momentum going. For Q4 FY '25,
