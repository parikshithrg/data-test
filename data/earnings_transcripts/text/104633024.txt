Rs.167.6 Crores as compared to Rs.122.3 Crores in Q2 FY2020; this was up by 37% YoY.

doubled, EBITDA margins expanded by 7.1% to 21.7% as compared to 14.6% in the 
previous corresponding quarter. PAT was at Rs.19.2 Crores compared to Rs.7.6 Crores up 
by 152%, PAT margins was at 11.4% as compared to 6.2% in the corresponding quarter.
