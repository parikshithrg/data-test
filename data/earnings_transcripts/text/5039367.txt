The EBITDA was about Rs. 57 crores which grew by around 14% quarter-on-quarter, but

ended in March 22, the revenue from operations stood at around Rs. 1153 crores which grew by

205 crores. EBITDA margin for the year stood at 17.77%. The net profit stood at around Rs. 128 
crores degrowing by 2.3% year-on-year and PAT margins for the year stood at around 11.09%.

had good topline growth on a year-on-year and quarter-on-quarter basis owing to the higher 
prices as well as volume addition of PAP ramp up. The raw material prices and market volatility 
continue to remain under pressure due to the geopolitical situation. On the CAPEX front, PAP 
ramp up continues to be on track and we had now achieved a production of 300 plus metric tons 
per month while OAP and pharma intermediates projects both are on track with no significant 
delays. OAP phase 1 trial run has been successful and both OAP and pharma intermediate plants 
expected to commence production from quarter 2 FY23. In FY22, the direct and deemed exports
