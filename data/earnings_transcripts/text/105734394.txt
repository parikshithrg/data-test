operation and maintenance is approximately EUR112 million. The order was bagged from ENI

renewable segment.

floating solar project, which also happens to be one of the single-largest floating solar blocks 
awarded till date in the country. The scope of the project includes modules and EPC work and 
the project is located in the state of Jharkhand.
