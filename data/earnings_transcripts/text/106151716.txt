Amit Saraf: 
So, see, we have received these questions several number of times from our individual investors. 
So, I want to provide some color on the lease liabilities which is appearing on the balance sheet. 
Under Ind AS 116, leases are not considered as traditional debt. Rather, there is an accounting 
adjustment that recognizes the present value of future lease payment as a liability.  
This adjustment is required under Ind AS 116 to ensure that lease obligations are transparently 
presented in the financial statement. The payments are split into principal repayment, reducing 
lease liability and interest expense. In other words, the lease liability reflects future rent payment 
as per lease contracts rather than an increase in financial debt.
