FY26. The margin improved to 17.1%, higher by almost 220 basis points 
quarter-on-quarter.

crores in Q3 FY26, reflecting a 117% quarter-on-quarter growth. Profit in 
the consolidated accounts has been impacted by around Rs. 10.4 crores 
due to elimination of profit of Rs. 5.9 crores from the subsidiaries and loss

for FY '26, that is higher by 15% year-on-year compared to Rs. 559.56
