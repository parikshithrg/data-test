During Q3FY25, the revenue declined YoY by 11% and increased QoQ by 8%. As on 9MFY25, 
the company registered a revenue of Rs.1,813 crore, the revenue achieved is similar to 9MFY24

projects such as Fintech building at GIFT city, Human and biological science gallery, GBRC,

However, during Q3FY25, the work has begun at these projects and the revenue has been 
booked. The profitability has dipped during the quarter due to additional expenses booked on 
UP projects.
