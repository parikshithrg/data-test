62 crores net of capital subsidy amounting INR 17 crores till 31st March '25. Our company 
continues to be net debt free and due to working capital efficiency, reported investments of INR 
357 crores as of 31st March '25. 
 
We have implemented key initiatives including the Relaxo Parivaar app, Brand As Seller model 
and broadening our e-commerce product offering. We are confident that these initiatives will
