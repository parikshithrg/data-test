a BF pellet. So for example, today, a BF pellet premium for a 65% Fe pellet is hardly about $13 
whereas for a DR pellet, the premium is as high as about $28. So that is where the difference is 
technically between a BF pellet and DR pellet.

of making the DR pellets and exporting to Middle East. We always have the option to doing 
that. But at the moment, looking at the current domestic demand, we will keep making BF pellets
