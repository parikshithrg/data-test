customers has led to CASA percentage staying above 27% for 2 consecutive quarters. We have 
witnessed improvements both on month-end balances and monthly average balances. With the 
planned enhanced engagements with CASA customers, we are poised to better this ratio. 
Cost of funds continue to trend lower due to the deposit rate cuts already taken in H1 FY '26 and 
better overall liquidity planning. Cost of funds for the quarter was 7.08% down 26 bps Q-o-Q.

at 165.6% as of December ‘25. Effective January 9, savings account deposit rates in the lowest

performance across unsecured and secured products.
