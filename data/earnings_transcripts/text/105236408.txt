Coming to Union Bank’s business & financials for quarter ended December 31st, 2021.  
The Bank has continued to post strong financials as net interest income grew by 8.88 % YoY; 
the net interest income stood at Rs. 7174 crore for Q3 of FY2022. The Global NIM stood at 3% 
for Q3 of FY2022. The Net Profit stood at Rs. 1085 crore during Q3 compared to the Rs. 727 
crore in Q3 FY 2021, thereby registering a growth of 49.29 %.

loan book to 56.94%. Within RAM sector, the retail advances grew 9.78% YoY and agriculture
