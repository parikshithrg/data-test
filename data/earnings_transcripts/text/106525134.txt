utilization for the segment stood at 66%. Revenue and EBITDA for the segment stood at Rs. 382 
crores and Rs. 58 crores respectively. EBITDA margin for the segment stood at about 15.3%.

segment stood at about 38%. The segment reported the revenue and EBITDA of Rs. 103 crores
