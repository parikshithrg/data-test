higher product prices. The EBITDA for the quarter stood at 12.89 crores as compared to Rs.16.6 
crores in the corresponding previous period. The EBITDA margin stood at 5.7% a decline of 
287 basis points year-on-year mainly due to continued increased in raw material prices coupled 
with freight and other cause as explain earlier. The price increase on all our products to the tune 
of 6%-10% has been done from May 2022 onwards. The profit after tax at 3.83 crores in Q4 
FY22 as against Rs.6.99 crores in Q4 FY21.
