state-of-the-art manufacturing plants at Khushkhera in Rajasthan and Dharuhera in Haryana,

Paramount was a profit-making and dividend-paying company till FY 2008. We were at that

cables manufacturer in UK, established circa year 1837 with AEI Cables Limited.

which, in the absence of bank funding, the only fresh funding available to offset the losses was 
the equity injections by the promoters over these years. With the support of additional equity put

from INR316 crores in FY17 to more than INR614 crores in FY19, with a pack of INR29 crores.

to double our turnover with the resources put in by the promoters.
