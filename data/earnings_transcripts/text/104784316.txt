time of 48 hours against industry average of 8 to 10 working days. We have centralized data 
science backed underwriting model with a fully integrated CRM and a loan management system.

75% of the customers who are salaried and 25% of them who are self-employed. We have a very 
strong focus on home loans and it constitutes 92% of the loan book. The average ticket size is 
10.1 lakhs. On the liability side, the liquidity pipeline is very strong with positive ALM. We 
have relationship with 17 banks and it is a good mix of institutions like public sector banks, 
private sector banks, and national housing bank. We are rated by ICRA and CARE and have a 
long-term rating of A plus with a stable outlook.
