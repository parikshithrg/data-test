on NITI Aayog projections of exceeding 9% growth. This resurgence 
will be fuelled by 3 factors: number one, sustained government support; 
number two, advancement in technology; and number three, the 
expansion of the export market, all of which will propel the industry size 
to reach USD 14.5 billion by the close of FY28.
