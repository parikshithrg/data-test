our overseas business our focus is to drive customer diversification in the order book and 
hence mitigate our customer concentration risk. We also drive cost actions through 
insourcing and working capital optimization. These efforts are likely to lead to a gradual 
recovery in the overseas markets and improved financial performance in the medium term.
