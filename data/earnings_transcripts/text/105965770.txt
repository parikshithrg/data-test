company was incorporated in 1988, 9th March. We are majorly into commodity trading. Right 
now, we are at the end of the financial year '23. 
 
There are some policy change in the government and now we are shifting our mode from 
exports to imports and manufacturing and processing locally. We are focusing more on the 
domestic market, reducing our Reliance on exports, which accounted for approximately 22% 
of our revenue in Q1 FY '24.

deliver a solid performance. Looking ahead, we expect to see further improvements as we plan 
to commercialize our new plant in Kolkata, dedicated to pulses and grains. Within, I think, it

plant in Surat, for which we have already acquired land and it will be commissioned in the 
next financial year.

particularly given the expected growth in the domestic market. As the government is not much 
keen on exports of agri-commodity, seeing our population is increasing and government wants 
to make ethanol from agricultural grains. They have started many plants. Government is 
encouraging to start many plants for manufacturing of ethanol from corn and all that. Corn, 
Sugarcane. What we feel that we won't be able to focus more on exports.
