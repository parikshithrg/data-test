Summary 
• Consolidated Revenue Y-o-Y decreased by 3.0% in USD terms and increased by  9.5% in INR terms, consolidated 
operating revenue in USD terms decreased by 0.4% in current quarter, over previous quarter. In INR terms, 
consolidated revenues increased  by 10.1% over previous quarter . 
• In USD terms, consolidated (excluding 3D PLM) operating revenues for the quarter decreased by 1.5% in USD 
terms and increased by  9.2 % in INR terms. It decreased by  6.0 %  Y-o-Y, in terms of USD & increased  by 6.5% 
in INR terms. 
• Effective tax rate for the quarter is 36.9% . 
• EPS for the quarter at INR 2.62 is higher by 7.4% over previous quarter primarily on account of higher 
contribution. Over corresponding quarter in previous year, it is lower by 8.0%. 
• EPS after adjusting for extraordinary  items is  INR 2.67 as against  INR 1.62 in Q1 14 and INR 3.98 in Q2 13. 
• New contracts amounting to USD 5.03 Mn awarded during the quarter. 
• Total headcount is decreased  from 4581 in Q1 14 to 4428 in Q2 14. 
• Salary increases in Geometric Excl 3DPLM effective 1st July 2013. 
• Losses booked in 3cap due to one time prior period adjustments. Impact of the same is about Euros 400k.

Note from the MD’s Desk 
 
While the quarter has been average, I am encouraged by the progress we are making 
in building a scalable enterprise. We have taken solid steps, some of which are 
already paying dividends. 
 
We have moved to a vertical structure with each vertical responsible for its own P&L. 
This has driven decision making downwards and brought a focus on performance. 
Each vertical has prepared both short term and long term action plans. The Power of 
Two structure has built a greater ownership between sales and delivery, and there is 
now increased attention to the existing customer base, which in turn will lead to 
greater sales. One indicator in this regard is our pipeline for new categories of 
business from existing customers increased by almost 50% in the last quarter, and 
the overall pipeline is up by over 30% in the same period. 
 
We have embarked on a journey to upgrade our go-to-market approach. This covers 
two aspects viz. sales process and sharper offerings. Changing the sales process is 
being done in a structured manner with the help of a global organisation. The 
process starts with evaluating the salesforce, followed by remedial training and 
coaching as appropriate. At the same time a methodology of monitoring and 
reviewing progress at each account and major opportunity within each account is 
being defined, and will be implemented over the next six months. As an outcome we 
should see a continued increase in pipeline, more systematic ‘mining’ of accounts, 
and greater deal closure. 
 
With the help of external consultants, we have launched an effort to revamp our 
current offerings with the dual objective of making them sharper and more focused, 
at the same time enhancing the size and scope, thus enabling us to make larger 
‘proposals’ to customers. The larger scope means we take more ‘end to end’ 
responsibility – which is what our customers are asking us to do. 
 
You may recall we began a journey in April to revamp our IT backbone. We have 
made good progress and we are still more or less on schedule to go live starting 
January 2014. The re-implementation involves using the ERP system ‘out of the box’ 
as against its currently heavily customized version. We believe this will help

Indeed the reimplementation has caused us to change our organisation structure,
