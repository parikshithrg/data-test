Adi Godrej 
Good afternoon everyone. A warm welcome to all of you to Godrej Properties Q4 & 
FY2010 conference call. I will take you through performance highlights of the 
period under review followed by discussion of the financial performance. Before we 
come to the highlights, as this is our first call after IPO in January I would like to 
take this opportunity to give you a brief introduction to Godrej Properties. We 
started operations in Mumbai in 1991 and later expanded into other cities. We are 
currently engaged in developing residential, commercial, and township projects 
across India. Our core strength lies in our joint venture business model under 
which we enter into development agreement with the landowners to acquire 
development rights for their land in exchange for a predetermined portion of 
revenues, profits or developable area generated from the project. This model is 
highly capital efficient and allows us to rapidly grow the business without tieing up 
large amounts of capital in land purchases. The merits of the model were proved 
during the downturn in 2008-2009 where Godrej Properties was one of the only 
real estate companies to report stable profits. One key factor that allows us to be 
successful in adding joint venture developments is the “Godrej” brand. 470 million 
Indians use Godrej product everyday. Having been in the business since 1897 the 
Godrej group is well known for its emphasis on corporate governance and 
professional and ethical business practices. The trust in the brand allows 
landowners to confidently partner with Godrej Properties for the development of 
their most valuable asset. Godrej Properties currently has projects in 10 major 
cities across India with a total of 82 million square feet of estimated developable 
area under various stages of planning or construction. We recognize the 
importance of delivering quality projects on a timely basis and to help us achieve 
this we have emplaced strategic partnerships for construction and design. For

instance, for construction we have an MOU with Larsen & Toubro whereby they will 
be the contractor for all our projects across the country. 
One of the highlights for the year was that we delivered 3.9 million square feet in 
financial year 2010. This compares to a total of 3.7 million square feet delivered 
from the Company's inception in 1991 until financial year 2009. Fiscal year 2010 
also witnessed considerable momentum in sales. We launched our first state of the 
art township Godrej Garden City in Ahmedabad in March 2010. The project 
received an overwhelming response and within days of its launch, a first phase of 
1.3 million square feet was completely booked. We are optimistic that subsequent 
launches will receive similar traction turning the project into a huge success. Apart 
from this, our other projects also fared well with significant booking being done in 
several recently launched projects. For instance, Godrej Prakriti, a residential 
development in Kolkata has seen bookings of 0.2 million square feet since its 
launch in Q3 financial year 2010. During the period under review, we had three 
private equity deals worth Rs. 156 Crore. Two of the deals were with HDFC PMS 
for our Chennai and Chandigarh projects and the third deal was with Milestone 
Real Estate Fund for our residential projects in Kolkata. Divesting stakes in certain 
projects through private equity is an ongoing part of our business strategy of 
monetizing assets at an appropriate stage and utilizing the proceeds to fund 
existing and new projects. 
 
Moving into the financial performance for the year, our consolidated total income 
for the year stood at Rs. 456 Crore, this is 53% higher than our total income of Rs. 
298 Crore in FY09. For the fourth quarter, total income increased by 140% to Rs. 
244 Crore from Rs. 101 Crore in the corresponding period last year. This significant 
increase is primarily driven by our projects in Ahmedabad, Kolkata, and Mumbai as 
well as by the private equity deals we concluded. For the year, EBITDA amounted 
to Rs. 231 Crore, a growth of 42% over the Rs. 163 Crore of EBITDA reported last 
year. EBITDA more than doubled for the fourth quarter and stood at 89 Crore as 
compared to 41 Crore in FY09. For FY10, net profit grew by 64% to Rs. 123 Crore 
from Rs. 75 Crore last year. For the quarter, net profit at Rs. 57 Crore grew by 
218% from Rs. 18 Crore in Q4 FY09. We are excited about the coming quarters in 
which we expect to see substantial momentum in terms of launches, deliveries, 
and new business developments. On this note, I conclude my remarks and would 
like to thank you all once again for joining us on this conference call. We will now 
be happy to discuss any questions, comments or suggestions that you may have.
