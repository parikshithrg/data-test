Let me start today by providing a brief overview of the quarter gone by and how we 
navigated the external challenges during this period: 
Quarter 1, while our year-on-year performance remained resilient, we witnessed a moderation 
in sequential performance, largely due to demand deferment arising from ongoing tariff 
uncertainties. The impact was more visible in export markets where customers took a cautious 
approach, particularly in terms of inventory stocking for their key raw materials. This caution 
coupled with subdued global manufacturing activity, I would say, temporarily affected our 
order book and our order flow.  
Despite these headwinds, we continue to focus on margins and customer-first approach. 
Consolidated Domestic:Export sales mix stood at 55:45 during the quarter. Our balance 
presents across domestic and export markets gives us the flexibility that is valued in times like 
these.
