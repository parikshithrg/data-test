over the same quarter last year. The lower rate of growth in consolidated PAT as compared to the 
growth in EBITDA is mainly on account of elimination of profit on intercompany transfer of 
certain intangible assets and the effect of tax thereon during the current quarter and the profit on 
sale of Cyclo business in the first quarter of last financial year.
