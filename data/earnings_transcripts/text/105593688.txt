services.

deployment of electric smart meters across India.

better. And going forward, most of the business will come through either the Platform or 
AMISP. 
 
The Bihar project is on track and revenue inflow from this project has started gaining traction. 
The Assam project is expected to commence by Q3 FY24.

against Q1 FY23, revenue of INR187 crores. Though the supply chain interruptions for
