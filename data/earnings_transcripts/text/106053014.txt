Our base business EBITDA margins were steady at 34%, similar to Q2 FY24. On a consolidated 
basis, our EBITDA margins for the quarter were 21%, impacted mainly by Cenexi, as previously 
mentioned. We continue to see healthy performance in our core markets of the United States, 
Europe, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. These key regions contributed 75% of our revenue 
in Q2 FY25, up from 74% in the same period last year.

future growth.

dynamics. Four of the nine products in the plan for the China market are currently under
