billets, binding wire, and scrap with a consistent focus on product quality and BIS compliance. 
Another important initiative is our 15-megawatt captive solar power plant which is currently 
under development. We see this as an important step towards improving our long-term energy 
economics, reducing cost, and strengthening our sustainability efforts.
