year-on-year increase of 31.7%. This growth underscores the resilience of our business model 
and our commitment to navigating through difficult market conditions. 
 
At the industry level, the organized footwear sector in India is well positioned to achieve mid-
teen growth in the medium term. This growth will be fuelled by continuous income increases 
and the gradual shift towards organized retail channels. Specifically, segments such as women's 
footwear, premium footwear, sports, and athleisure are expected to experience significant 
growth supported by an expanding presence of brand outlets.
