This quarter there has been a regulatory transition for the gold lending industry, which I expect will 
be a common theme across the sector this season. Effective April 1, the RBI revised the LTV 
framework, requiring onboarding LTVs on bullet loans to be calculated on the total amount due at 
maturity — that is, principal and interest together — rather than on principal alone. In response, and 
to support our customers while maintaining prudent risk standards, we have adopted a periodic 
interest-due structure, and we continue to operate at LTVs below the regulatory limits. As customer 
repayment behaviour is unlikely to change immediately, reported overdue levels are expected to 
remain elevated in the near term. We view it as customer behaviour in transition rather than an asset 
quality concern.

The new guidelines have made bullet loans less attractive for customers. While this has necessitated
