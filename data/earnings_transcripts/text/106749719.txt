quarter, the nameplate capacity of Dahej increased from 17.5 MMTPA to 22.5 MMTPA. So the 
utilization numbers now reflect a significantly larger capacity base.

quarter and 90% in the previous quarter. Overall company capacity utilization in the current 
quarter was 58% compared to 76% in both the corresponding and previous quarters.
