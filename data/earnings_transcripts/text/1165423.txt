Business Landscape During The Quarter
Automotive ~ Q2FY15 revenues: USD 16.05 Mn; q-o-q growth 6.2%
• Led the volume growth for the quarter
• A joint engagement with an ISV Partner for a Chinese auto maker jointly for PLM 2.0 
and revitalization of the customer’s PLM environment   
• Started an important new PLM engagement with automotive OEM in Europe 
• IT spending freeze at a leading North American auto OEM resulted in muted growth 
in the quarter, which will continue for the remainder of CY 2014
Industrial ~ Q2FY15 revenues: USD 7.36 Mn; q-o-q growth 0.7%
• Recovery continues to be slow
• Mining Industry continues to struggle, as a result our customers are not engaging in 
new projects or investments
• Our Agricultural customers are also seeing a slowdown in sales and softening in their 
market, specifically in North America.
• Entered a new engagement with a Chinese customer for model based engineering
• Got selected as the ‘preferred supplier’ for drafting and drawing needs of an 
Agricultural major
• Started working with an Indian engineering major providing engineering services
3

Business Landscape During The Quarter
Emerging & Software ~ Q2FY15 revenues: USD 4.49 Mn; q-o-q growth 4.3%
• This vertical caters to long standing steady engagements. Working on enhancing 
these engagements with more senior level customer connects and adding more value
to the customers 
• Positive inroads made due to this strategy
• Increased penetration at a Japanese customer by engaging with new divisions and 
verticals of the customer offering them an end-to-end engineering solution
• Began a new software product development initiative with a PLM ISV for their next 
generation software
4
Aerospace  ~ Q2FY15 revenues: USD 2.81 Mn; q-o-q drop of 7%
• While highest percentage growth anticipated from this vertical, we saw a drop in Q2 
revenues
• Decision delayed on two major contracts with a European and a North American 
customer that were expected in Q1/Q2; decision anticipated in Q3 end, which will 
show benefit only in Q4FY15 
• On the positive side, working on several should costing solution pilots for various 
companies of an American Aerospace conglomerate 
• Positive feedback and interest seen in a new Build to Spec offering jointly provided 
with an Indian aerospace supplier 

Business Landscape During The Quarter

Geometry Technology Solutions  ~ Q2FY15 revenues: USD 2.49 Mn; q-o-q growth 
12.8%
• Healthy growth and uptick in our products business has helped in improving our 
bottom line
• Growth driven by enterprise sales of Geometric DFx and MBE solution. Good 
traction seen for DFx across our customer base and are currently working on 
multiple pilots auto majors, aerospace companies, and high tech companies
• Enterprise sales well supported by stronger channel sales, primarily for CAMWorks
and Glovius
• Strengthened the enterprise and channel sales engine
• Innovation is the core of this business and we are currently exploring software 
solution for 3DPrinting

and traction shows the soundness of the plan, albeit the growth is not at the pace we desire.  
 
Our results were affected by four factors, one of which was anticipated. We increased our 
salaries effective 1st July, which had an adverse impact on margins as we had forecast last 
quarter. While Geometric Technology Solutions (GTS) grew around 13% Q-on-Q, and 
Automotive by over 6% Q-on-Q; our Industrial unit was relatively flat and Aerospace declined 
in revenue Q-on-Q mainly on account of delays in finalizing new contracts at two major 
customers - resulting in a below anticipated growth in revenues. Our billed utilization at just 
over 75%, was well below budget for reasons we have ascertained and are correcting. Finally, 
as other IT companies have noted, the Euro weakened significantly, which resulted in an 
erosion of margins. 
 
Thus, three of the factors affected the margins and one affected the topline. As we look to the 
next quarter, there will be no further increase in salaries this FY; we see improvement in 
revenue for all our units; and we are taking steps to improve our utilization significantly, all of 
which should see an improvement in margins, profits and growth. 
 
We are now looking at enhancing our offerings. We see the PLM software world changing 
significantly. There is a move from ‘point to point’ solutions to an approach which embraces 
the enterprise, not only involving software, but requiring the integration with both mechanical 
and embedded systems engineering services. This is a shift we had long anticipated, which is 
why Geometric invested in Mechanical Engineering Services in 2006 and Embedded Systems 
in 2013. Thus, the coming shift in technology in the next five years provides us an opportunity 
which we are uniquely positioned to benefit from.  We not only have the skill set, but also   a 
customer base, which comprises of large companies who are likely to be the early adopters 
as they would like to see the benefits of integrated solutions. To ensure we participate in the 
opportunity we have created a Technical Council to guide us in building the right strategy, skill 
sets and offerings. The strategy and approach centers around leveraging what we have and 
build on it, while identifying gaps which we need to fill either by internal investment or 
through acquisition. 
 
Finally, while I see continued acceleration in our rate of growth excluding our JV, it is clear to 
me we will be unable to meet the NASSCOM industry growth of 13-15% this year. I put this 
down to delays in some major contracts in the Aerospace unit, which we had hoped to win 
coupled with slower than expected growth in one or two units. While none of the situations 
are irretrievable, the delay has an adverse impact.
