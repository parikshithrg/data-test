small parks spread over a few acres.

standardization, safety, and customer experience. Building and operating large format parks

naturally creates high entry barriers.

pay for a high quality experience, pricing has to remain relevant to the catchment and local 
demographics. Beyond a certain point, higher ticket prices can impact affordability and footfalls.

industry characteristics create a significant opportunity for organized players like 
Imagicaaworld.
