Let me first put the perspective of the power sector: 
Last year the growth of power demand for the whole year has been nearly 5%, and in 
the March quarter it was nearly 4%. And we expect that this year also the growth will

270 gigawatts. And the way the power sector is geared up, I think we will be able to 
meet that additional load. From Tata Power’s side also, all our plants will be operating 
at full capacity, so that whatever is our commitment in terms of supply we will be able 
to meet.

Q4 EBITDA has also increased 14% to Rs. 3,829 crores compared to last year's Rs. 
3,358 crores.
