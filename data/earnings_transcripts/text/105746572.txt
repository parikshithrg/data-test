fiscal with robust bid pipeline suggesting further awards in the current fiscal year. We believe 
that India will emerge as one of the fastest growing infrastructure drivers in the world. Marked 
by an unprecedented investment going into virtually every part of its transforming infrastructure 
story, whether it is for airports, ports, railway stations, metros, expressway, water treatment 
projects or ropeways. 
 
On the other hand, we believe that a sectoral shakeout, higher construction benchmarks and 
rising prequalification standards have led to an industry-wide consolidation. These strengths 
Favor larger companies with robust balance sheet, enabling them to address even bigger 
opportunities and strengthen their market position. 
 
Coming to the performance highlights for Q3 FY '24. J. Kumar's revenue from operations for 
Q3 FY '24 grew by 15% to INR1,219 crores as compared to INR1,062 crores in Q3 FY '23. The 
operating margin for Q3 FY '24 grew by 18% to INR179 crores as compared to INR152 crores 
in the preceding year.
