The future. Dear All, we would like to recall the roadmap of the company presented in our last 
call that is INR20,000 crores of AUM and GNPA below 2% by 2027. With the problems which

having been overcome, the company is well and truly on its way to reach these numbers. For the 
current year, the company plans to strengthen its resources in sales, collection and underwriting 
verticals by market recruitments. We will be adding another 40 outlets in FY '25 to make the 
number of outlets to 250. 
 
Two more regions would be added to our present 9 to make deliveries faster. Topping the current 
year agenda are growth acceleration and overdue reductions. With the momentum gained in the 
just concluded year and the learnings, the company is confident of reaching a disbursement of 
INR3,600 crores to INR3,800 crores, an AUM of INR15,000 crores, stage 2 numbers between
