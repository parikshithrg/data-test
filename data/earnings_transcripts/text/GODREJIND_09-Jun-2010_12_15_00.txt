results for the financial year 2010. I will begin this call by taking you through our 
business model as well as our key economic interests. 
Our unique business model is diversified by virtue of having both, a combination of 
operating business and several economic interests across key group entities, 
which expose our stakeholders to some of the fastest growing sectors in the Indian 
economy. Our activities encompass chemicals, agriculture, real estate, FMCG and 
food segments. This diversity has allowed us to achieve steady growth in the past 
and we expect impressive growth in our businesses going forward. Our incumbent 
chemicals business has maintained its market leadership position in India in oleo-
chemicals and surfactant industries. 
In the chemicals division we use products ranging from fatty acids, fatty alcohol, to 
specialty fatty acids and surfactants. The business caters to the needs of personal 
care, home care and specialized applications. Specialty fatty acids and surfactants 
are specialized products which require a high level of technological perfection in 
manufacturing and quality control and hence command good margins. The aim of 
the business is also to maximize operational efficiency by reducing networking 
capital, conversion costs and fixed costs, while improving yields. 
We have introduced a customer relationship management website to provide our 
customers with information regarding orders and transactions. This is a part of our 
ongoing effort to improve our business through innovation and technology. This is a 
very stable business and our efforts to improve efficiencies will continue to yield 
results in the coming year. We are also excited about our range of economic 
interest, in particular across realty, agriculture and FMCG space which continue to 
demonstrate great growth potential. 
Godrej Agrovet, our agri business covers animal feed, agricultural inputs, oil palm 
plantation and poultry. The agri space offers exciting opportunities in the Indian 
context and we believe that we are very well placed in this sector given our brand, 
quality, manufacturing and distribution reach. We believe that over the medium to 
long term, the efforts and investments we have put in the past and in the present 
will make visible contribution to the sustained performance of this business. Godrej 
Agrovet had a successful financial year with several new product launches and 
initiatives including a seaweed extract that improves crop yields. Venturing into 
providing quality maize seeds to farmers, as part of our effort to be a one-stop 
solution provider to Indian farmers. Godrej Agrovet, along with its subsidiaries and 
joint ventures is India’s largest crude palm oil producer.

The oil palm business operates on a command area model with state governments 
allocating specific territories to corporates for the development of oil palm 
plantations. Growers are required to sell the entire production of fresh fruit bunches 
at a predetermined price linked to output price set by the government. Currently we 
have about 37,000 hectares of area under cultivation out of which about 12,000 
hectares is fruit bearing. The oil palm business has gained critical mass; an 
operating maturity is likely to be a key value driver for the company in the future. 
Godrej Properties, our real estate business was successfully listed on the stock 
exchanges in January and was received very positively. Our real estate business 
utilizes a highly capital efficient model that allows us to quickly grow the business 
without tying out large amounts of capital in land purchases. There are currently 
projects in 10 cities across India with 82 million sq. ft. of upcoming developable 
area, which includes residential, commercial and township developments. Sales 
have been outstanding this year especially with regard to our first state of the art 
township Godrej Garden City in Ahmedabad. The project was launched in March 
and received an overwhelming response resulting in the booking of 1.3 million sq. 
ft. within days. This demand underlines optimism for Godrej Properties, which I 
believe will experience significant growth this decade. One of the key highlights for 
the year was that Godrej Properties delivered 3.9 million sq. ft. in financial year 
2010 as compared to a total of 3.7 million sq. ft. delivered since inception in 1991 
till financial year 2009. 
Godrej Consumer Products, our main FMCG business had a very impressive year 
with several major events. GCPL has signed an agreement to acquire Sara Lee 
stake in Godrej Sara Lee which will make GCPL India’s largest homegrown and 
personal care company and we are likely to conclude this transaction today. In 
addition, GCPL has made acquisitions outside India acquiring the Megasari Group, 
a leader in Indonesian household care and agreeing to acquire Tura a leading 
Nigerian personal care company, a leader in the hair color segment in Argentina 
and with strong presence in other Latin American countries. There have been new 
product launches in Godrej #1 toilet soap, Godrej Renew Hair Colourants and 
Cinthol Deodorant Sprays. Godrej Protect, a line of hand-hygiene products have 
been introduced with hand sanitizers, hand washes and hand wipes.  
Before we start the Q&A session, let me quickly run you through our financial 
highlights during the year. 
Our consolidated income increased by 5% to Rs. 3781 crores from Rs. 3614 
crores. EBITDA increased 25% to Rs. 413 crores from Rs. 328 crores and net
