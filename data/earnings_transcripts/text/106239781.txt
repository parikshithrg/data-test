versus Rs. 7,822 crores in Financial Year ‘24. This is because of the increased sales across all 
the segments. Gross profit stood at Rs. 3,030 crores and the gross profit margins expanded by 
200 bps from 32.5% to 34.5%. EBITDA increased by 8% to Rs. 1,067 crores compared to Rs. 
988 crores last year. EBITDA margin was at 12.2% that is marginally lower by 40 bps. The
