guidelines that we have to select one land valuer by POWERGRID and one valuer will be 
by the farmers or the landowners and the two rates will be given by valuers. Average of 
these two rates will be paid to the farmers. So that way, we are resolving the RoW issues.

Rajasthan. In Delhi and Haryana, we are in the process of resolving RoW issues. But still 
there are challenges, still issues unresolved especially in Delhi and Haryana. So positive 
action is on the ground. And we are able to commission transmission line. But there have
