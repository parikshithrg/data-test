annuity-based assets such as industrial and logistics park, commercial space and leasing 
developed land for CFS/ICD private freight terminal operations.  
 
The Company has the necessary capabilities and domain expertise and understands the local 
nuances specific to the industry sectors and regions. The Company has presence across major 
cities in India. It had successfully developed 5.5 million square feet and exited 4.8 million square 
feet of Grade A projects in Delhi NCR, that is the Farukhnagar, Bengaluru at Malur, Hyderabad 
at Patancheru, and Goa at Venkatpura.

assets across India. The Company may also explore new opportunities in alternative asset class 
like MMLP, plotted development, etc. We have shared the details of our upcoming projects in
