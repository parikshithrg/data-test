sense that while the quality of primary or secondary is the same just as an example a beverage 
can scrap will go into beverage can.A scrap coming from auto shredding will go into production 
of auto alloys. A scrap from extrusion will go into production of billets for auto production. So 
the restriction is only to this extent that we must segregate the scrap for its right application to 
get the maximum value. Otherwise secondary aluminium can be used practically for everything. 
While on the other hand a primary aluminium a 99.7% pure aluminium which still needs to be 
alloyed can be used universally for any application. So that is why primary aluminium prices are 
higher and secondary are comparatively cheaper. And this has been the case in the past and I 
believe this would be the case in future as well.
