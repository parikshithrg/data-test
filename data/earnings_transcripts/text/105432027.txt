improved to ₹315 crores in December '22. Net debt has declined from ₹ 
336 crores to ₹ 260 crores. Consequently, net debt stood at 0.83 of 
equity.

softening raw material prices and easing supply chain bottlenecks, we 
have been able to bring down our working capital by maintaining 
optimal raw material and finished goods stocks. Our continued efforts 
have allowed the company to reduce working capital outlay by ₹ 85 
crores on a quarter-on-quarter basis. 
I would like to reiterate that the company has delivered a five-year 
CAGR revenue and PAT growth of 20% and 35% respectively.
