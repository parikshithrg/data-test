On a consolidated basis, revenue from operations increased to 27.8% year-over-year to INR

improved by 29.3% year-over-year to INR 836 per sheet, supported by an improved product 
mix. The business reported the EBITDA of INR 52 million with an EBITDA margin of 7%.

representing a 25% substantial increase, while the total sales volume increased to 20.4% 
subsequently to approximately 1.13 lakh sheets. The business reported EBITDA of INR 23 
million, translating into an EBITDA margin of 20.6%, in line with the management expectation. 
The improving contribution from the Jumbo laminate supported the overall performance of the 
laminate business. We continue to see increasing acceptance of our Jumbo laminate offerings 
across the international markets, which remains an important area of focus as we work towards 
improving capacity utilization.
