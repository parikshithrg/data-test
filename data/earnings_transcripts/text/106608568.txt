AI does not primarily create value by upgrading systems - it creates value by upgrading

generated, and how costs are managed.

the critical enabler: providing secure architecture, robust data foundations, integration

to business functions, where AI-driven decisions directly translate into measurable 
outcomes.

marketing, and supply chain.
