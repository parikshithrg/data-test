On the industry performance, India's macroeconomic environment during Q3 FY 26 
remains supportive for the automotive sector with growth visible across all major 
segments.  
As per SIAM production data for Q3 FY 26, Passenger Vehicle (PV) production was 
up by 19% year-on-year (y-o-y), 2-wheelers were up by 15% y-o-y, 3-wheelers at 
roughly 3.4 lakh units were up by 35% y-o-y; and Commercial Vehicles (CV also saw 
a positive upward trend of 18% y-o-y.  
This broad-based expansion underscores the strong momentum across the industry 
with overall robust performance of OEMs during the quarter. 
 
Importantly, domestic passenger vehicle sales grew by an impressive 21% y-o-y in Q3 
FY 26 compared to the same quarter last year. Demand was further aided by the 
benefits of recent GST rationalization, a buoyant festive and marriage season and 
continued premiumization trends across different categories.
