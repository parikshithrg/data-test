Note from the MD’s Desk 
 
The last twelve months have been focused on stabilizing and preparing the Company by 
‘Changing the Gears’. While there is a lot more to do to build a sustainable growth engine, I 
honestly feel we are seeing the ‘light at the end of the tunnel’. 
 
In the last two quarters, we have begun to bid for multi-million dollars, multiyear projects. 
Such transactions tend to draw on management bandwidth, and are prone to delays, as they 
cover a multitude of functions across the customer’s enterprise, some of which have diverse 
priorities. The fact is that had we not invested in changing our sales model, we would not 
have been able to bid for these transactions or would have missed them. The secret lies in 
getting a sufficient number in the pipeline, so as to minimize the effects of delay in 
individual transactions impacting growth. In this context, our pipeline has grown by 57% 
over last year, and the opportunities >$500K have grown by 73% in value and 33% in 
number over the same period in the prior year. 
 
Also, we have begun an exercise to better leverage our own IP by increasing investment in 
both sales and product development. Our objective here is two-fold, (1) increase our 
differentiator and our ability to add value to our customers in a unique manner; while at the 
same time (2) improve our margins through our ability to support an element of non-linear 
growth. Towards this, we are pursuing new avenues of product development and are 
working on several innovative areas, which we hope to launch during the course of this 
financial year. 
 
In addition, we recognize the need to bring more rigor to our financial reporting, especially 
as we continually increase our global presence. Starting the coming quarter, all our overseas 
subsidiaries will be audited by a single firm, one of the big four. This will ensure a consistent 
approach, which will translate into better accuracy, as well as timeliness in our reporting. 
 
The SAP reimplementation continues to face some challenges, and it is fair to say we have 
not derived the desired benefits yet. Nonetheless, we believe we are on the right track, and 
in the coming quarter, we should be able to get data from the system in a more timely 
manner, minimizing the use of excel sheets. There is no doubt that to achieve success in the 
reimplementation, we have to improve our discipline, another essential ingredient in 
building a scalable enterprise.  
 
In closing, I believe, we are on the right track. We should see growth improve in the next 
quarter, and we continue to build on some meaningful relationships, which will further 
support the growth and margin engines. For the next quarter, an improvement in the 
growth rate is anticipated, but the wage increase effective 1st July will impact margins. 
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
Manu Parpia  
Managing Director & CEO

Income statement provided is in the MIS format.
MIS format is different from the income statement published as part of financial results in 
terms of grouping of cost elements.

hardware (CAPEX equivalent) and excludes software depreciation which is clubbed

in financials.
4.
MIS Format provides only interest expenses whereas financial results clubs interest 
and bank charges in one line item.

have been reduced in depreciation
