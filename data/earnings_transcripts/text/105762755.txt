hotels. Standalone EBITDA for Q3 were INR19.79 crore as 
compared to INR17.36 crores, an increase of 14%. Standalone 
PAT before exceptional items of Q3 stood at INR9.28 crores as 
compared to INR8.2 crores in Q3 last year, an increase of 13%.
