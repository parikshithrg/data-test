Talking about technology and AI initiatives, I should say at Kissht, technology is not a 
department in the company, rather it is the company. We are a technology company that lends, 
not a lender that has bolted technology on the side and that distinction has only sharpened as

underwriting stack reads more than 7,200 signals on each borrower. This includes bureau data,
