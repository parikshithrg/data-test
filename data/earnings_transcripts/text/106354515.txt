OMERAS is a company with an illustrious 187-year heritage in Germany, specializing in 
architectural facades, vitreous enamel coating, and advanced metal processing.Through 
OMERAS Store, it also markets glass fused to steel tanks and silos, ranging from 8 cubic 
meters to over 20,000 cubic meters in capacity. These modular voltage tanks are widely used 
for storage of potable water, wastewater sludge, industrial liquids, animal feedstocks, biogas, 
and grain. OMERAS has successfully delivered 350 tanks in Saudi Arabia alone in the past
