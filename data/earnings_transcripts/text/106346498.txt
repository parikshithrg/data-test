complement our process moat, electron-beam welding and diffusion bonding, which gives us control of

Our performance highlights this quarter underscore that strategy. EBITDA grew 32.5% with a 452 bps

demand, while Rest of Asia rose 62.81% albeit from a lower base, offsetting softer exports in the West.

down 29 to 212.

carry net cash of ₹77 crore: supporting forward integration, automation and capacity debottlenecking 
without stretching capital.
